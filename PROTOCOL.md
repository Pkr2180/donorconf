# Analysis protocol (DRAFT — not registered)

Working title: *Donor-Level Conformal Calibration for Single-Cell Foundation Models Under Dataset Shift*

Status: **draft written after development-time simulations were seen.** The hypotheses below were
revised in light of them (section 1). The real-data confirmatory analysis must be locked and
time-stamped (e.g. OSF) **before any real target dataset is touched**. Until then nothing here is
pre-registered, and simulation results are exploratory.

## 0. Estimands

* **E1 (donor coverage).** For a new donor drawn from the target population, the expected fraction of
  its cells whose true label lies in the prediction set, averaged with **equal weight per donor**.
  Nominal target 1 − α.
* **E2 (composition).** For cell type k and donor groups g1, g0, the difference between groups in the mean
  per-donor proportion of type k.
* Cells are never the sampling unit. A cell-pooled coverage figure is reported only as a comparison.

## 1. What the development simulations changed

| Original expectation | What the simulations showed | Consequence for the paper |
|---|---|---|
| Cell-pooled conformal under-covers new donors | Approximately nominal on average when donor size is unrelated to difficulty; under-covers when large donors are easier and donor heterogeneity is high; a within-dataset random cell split reports nominal coverage regardless | Claim narrowed to the conditions where it occurs, with the reported-vs-realised gap as the headline |
| Donor-level CRC is the fix | Valid, but sets are much larger than needed with <~25 donors and vacuous below 1/(n+1) | Add the donor-weighted quantile as the efficient companion; frame as a validity/efficiency trade-off |
| The shift screen protects users | Detects large feature offsets; did **not** discriminate coverage-failing from coverage-safe target sets under concept shift; 3-donor recalibration did not repair it | Do not call it a certificate. Report as a detector of feature shift only unless H2 passes on real data |
| Donor-level PPI beats DCATS | A confusion-matrix proxy (not DCATS) was competitive; PPI had narrower intervals at small donor counts | DCATS itself must be run before any claim |

Full numbers: `results/SUMMARY.md` (generated by `scripts/summarize_results.py`).

**Published-package baseline (added 2026-09-21).** The conformalized single-cell annotator (López-De-Castro et al.,
*Bioinformatics* 2025, btaf521) calibrates with torchCP 1.0.2. Its `SplitPredictor` (THR, APS), `ClassWisePredictor`
and `ClusteredPredictor` were run directly (not re-implemented) on this study's head/splits
(`src/donorconf/baseline_torchcp.py`). Result: standard-THR is numerically identical to this study's own
cell-pooled method (cross-checked to < 0.002 disagreement on exchangeable data, and mean |difference| 0.0000–0.0013
on real data); classwise/clustered under-cover the same way this study's own classwise method does. This isolates
the calibration step only — the annotator's OOD detector and neural classifier are not included; state this
wherever tcp_* results are shown.

## 2. Hypotheses and pre-specified kill criteria (real data)

**H1 — new-donor coverage.** Hold out whole donors (and separately whole datasets). Compare cell-pooled
conformal, donor-weighted quantile, donor-level CRC, all at α = 0.10.
*Supported if* cell-pooled realised donor-mean coverage is more than 0.03 below nominal in at least
one prespecified fold family **or** its within-dataset reported coverage exceeds realised by more than 0.03.
*Not supported (paper reframed as a benchmark/negative result) if* cell-pooled stays within ±0.02 of
nominal and its poorly-covered-donor fraction is not larger than donor-level's.

**H2 — shift screen.** Type-I error ≤ 0.05 + Monte-Carlo slack; report power by shift type.
*Claim "screen predicts failure" only if* P(fail | flagged) − P(fail | not flagged) ≥ 0.15 on real
held-out datasets. Otherwise the screen is described as a feature-shift detector and the certify/abstain
framing is dropped.

**H3 — composition intervals.** On real data with a labeled donor subset: donor-level PPI interval
coverage within ±0.03 of nominal and width no larger than DCATS-based intervals in at least half of
prespecified contrasts. Otherwise drop layer 3 from the paper (keep the code).

**H4 — foundation-model embeddings.** Repeat H1 with at least two foundation-model embeddings and a PCA
baseline, to show the donor-level issue is a property of the data structure, not one model.
*Status (2026-09-21/22):* satisfied for two Census-hosted foundation-model embeddings (TranscriptFormer-Sapiens,
TranscriptFormer-Exemplar) plus scVI as a non-foundation-model baseline, across both blood and lung. No PCA-only
baseline was run (a PCA-64 step is inside the head for every embedding, not a separate embedding condition).
Geneformer/scGPT are not Census-hosted and were not run (need GPU extraction, out of scope for this version).

**H5 — second tissue (added 2026-09-21, run 2026-09-22).** Repeat the C benchmark (H1's reported-vs-realised gap
and the confidence-shift signal) on a tissue other than blood, using the same fixed inclusion rule
(`results/real/ANALYSIS_LOCK.md` Addenda C2/C3), decided and written down before any lung coverage number was
computed. *Supported if* the reported-vs-realised gap and the confidence-shift Spearman correlation (|ρ| ≥ 0.4)
both replicate in the second tissue. *Result:* supported — lung's gap is larger than blood's (71.5% vs 47.9% of
replications with |gap| > 0.03) and its confidence-shift Spearman is −0.776 (vs −0.732 in blood), both well past
the kill threshold. The permutation screen again does not predict shortfall (Spearman 0.106, p = 0.622 in lung).

## 3. Data rules

* Reference, calibration and target sets are **separate donors**; datasets are held out whole for the
  dataset-shift analysis. Donor ids are namespaced by dataset.
* Only cell-type labels assigned by the data's authors, independently of the model, are ground truth.
  Labels produced by the same foundation model are not.
* Cell types absent from the reference are dropped from calibration/target and counted (never relabelled).
* **Minimum donors.** Donor-level CRC needs α ≥ 1/(n+1): 10 calibration donors is the floor for α = 0.10,
  and sets stay inflated below about 25. Datasets below the floor are used for nothing but reporting.
* Each dataset's accession, download date, file hash and donor count are recorded in
  `config/datasets_manifest.csv` **before** analysis. The current manifest is unverified; several
  candidate datasets are too small (see WORKPLAN.md).

## 4. Analysis rules

* All thresholds, α, score types and donor groupings are fixed before evaluation. No test-guided tuning.
* Report coverage with a donor-bootstrap interval. Report set size beside every coverage figure.
* Report failure cases: any scenario where a method under-covers by more than 0.05 is shown, not omitted.
* Simulation and real-data results are kept in separate tables and never pooled.

## 5. Known limitations to state in the paper

* Donor-level guarantees are **expectations over donors**, not per-donor or per-cell conditional guarantees.
* Exchangeability of target and calibration donors is assumed; label-free checks cannot certify it.
* Composition PPI needs gold labels for a random subset of donors.
* The prediction-set guarantee concerns labels in the calibration label space; unseen cell types are not covered.
